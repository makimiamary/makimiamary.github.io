Speaking of the door, let's talk about how this beautiful room is put together.

✅ Apartment Residents

In the Apartment World, you can visit Henry's neighbors' rooms. While the rooms are "basically" built the same, the interiors and accessories of each room are different, allowing you to see into their respective lives.

The notes on the portraits in the painter's room also reveal the residents' personalities.

Of course, even within the same room, Henry's interior differs from that of past residents. I previously wrote an article about how these details can reveal which belongings are Henry's.

Room 303, Eileen's room...

It's well known that the painting and the skirt in the living room here also appear in SH3.


Whoa, Eileen's rich!

✅ Room 302

And then there's this iconic room.


Like the door, this kitchen and living room have been paid homage to in various games. Fan-made indie horror games "inspired by the Silent Hill series" often feature rooms modeled after this one.

Not only that, but recently it seems that some assets are based on this room, and the kitchen/living room combination can be seen in indie games as well.


These are the same asset. At first glance, they only appear to be similar and nothing more. But when you see them as a set, you can tell that Room 302 is the base.


It feels like there are quite a few creators who use the asset without even realizing it.

With today's graphics, even indie game assets are realistic, but I still love the design of this original room.

A sliding door that doesn't quite fit. A drawer that sticks. A sagging cushion. I love how much attention is paid to those small details, just as much as to stains, scratches, and signs of wear. I can really feel the creators' love in those details.




As I said about the door, these details in an old building are full of the "beauty of imperfection."

I've seen plenty of homages, fan art, and replicas too, but none of them were crafted to this extent. Nothing beats this room. It might be even more comfortable than my own home, haha.
