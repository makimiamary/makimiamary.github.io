A site examining overseas articles claiming that SH4 was originally a separate game called Room 302 before becoming part of the Silent Hill series. A claim absent from official Japanese sources.

It examines the credibility of these articles through screenshots, comparing cases where the same writer reported different figures and where different writers reported identical figures. It also traces how the rumor spread through the English-speaking community by comparing old forum discussions.

Many people still casually bring up this theory online as a "harmless bit of trivia", but I hope they'll learn about its roots and understand the harm these distorted claims can cause to the work, its devs, and its fans.

[Silent Hill 4] The Dubious Roots of the Room 302 Theory

Breaking Down the Original Source of a 20-Year Rumor with No Japanese Evidence

The figures change between articles by the same writer, yet match across articles by different writers. There are no photos of the interviewee, and the Q&A content is nearly identical, with each writer seeming to have rearranged and restructured it. The articles may have appeared to come from two independent sources. They may actually trace back to the same source.

This site breaks down the inconsistencies in the Eurogamer and Boomtown articles about SH4 that have circulated in the English-speaking community for over 20 years, using side-by-side image comparisons. It also presents the correct info from official Japanese sources.

　

Bias Against Asia Seen in Opposing Stories of SILENT HILL 4's Development

English Journalism and Orientalism in Gaming Media

Despite being a sensational interview that has circulated for over 20 years, there is nothing in the headline to highlight it. Was the ambiguity in the relevant parts meant to prevent pushback from the interviewee through an interpreter?

Using the Eurogamer and Boomtown articles on SH4, this article examines the bias toward English-language sources and the bias against Asia among those who trusted the English articles over the developers and the work itself.

　

[Silent Hill 4] The Mysterious "Game World" Article and a Community That Preferred Gossip

How Rumors Spread

A third article about an interview that now only survives on a fan site. The article was unreliable, with one of the devs denying another part of its claims.

As in the case of the two articles above, other sources were also reporting the official info and correct facts at the time. The community, though, seemed to favor the more sensational articles. And I also documented defenses of them.
