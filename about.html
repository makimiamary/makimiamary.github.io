About

Site Info

This site primarily documents and examines info about SH4, along with related topics, including articles, interviews, development history, in-game content, and claims that have circulated in the English-speaking community. It is a backup of the articles published on DeviantArt and Medium. The site creator is Japanese. English is their second language. All the platforms I use, including this one, are free personal accounts with no commercial purpose. I chose them because they're likely to remain available for a long time.

It includes research into the claim that SH4 was originally a separate game called Room 302 before becoming part of the Silent Hill series, a claim that was not present in Japanese sources, as well as analysis of the game itself.
